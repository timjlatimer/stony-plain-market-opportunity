The ambition

Imagine the West Edmonton Mall of farmers’ markets.

A Canadian showpiece for food, local enterprise and the brands that bring people together.

That is the ambition: a destination with the variety, energy and sense of discovery that makes a visit worth the trip. Anchor stores are intended to give visitors a reason to return throughout the week. The indoor market and covered outdoor stalls could bring another dimension on market weekends.

For your brand, the opportunity is to help shape the destination from the beginning—with a space, storefront and customer experience designed around you.

West Edmonton Mall is a reference for destination appeal, with no proposed affiliation. This is a development concept; operating performance is not established.

The market around you

A reason to visit.
More reasons to stay.

The concept brings three experiences together: anchor shopping, a large indoor farmers market, and outdoor trading under permanent matching roofs.

  1. The anchor experience

    Your proposed store can operate throughout the week, alongside the destination’s evolving market programme.

  2. The open indoor hall

    Approximately 75,000 sq ft for movable farmers-market stalls, food and makers. A single open volume, with no second floor, mezzanine or upper walkway.

  3. The covered outdoor market

    Stalls wrap all four exterior sides. A permanently roofed, open-sided market promenade extends toward the road, with stalls on both sides and required vehicle crossovers. Parking access is to be developed with the site plan.
